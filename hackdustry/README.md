### 📥 Installation

1. Download the latest release
2. Place `hackdustry.zip` in your Mindustry mods folder:
   - **Android**: `/sdcard/Android/data/io.anuke.mindustry/files/mods/`
   - **iOS**: `(Not supported on iOS)`
   - **Windows**: `%APPDATA%/Mindustry/mods/`
   - **Linux**: `~/.local/share/Mindustry/mods/`
   - **macOS**: `~/Library/Application Support/Mindustry/mods/`
3. Restart Mindustry
4. Look for the green **"H"** button in-game!

### 🎵 Adding Custom Music

1. Find the music folder:
   - Open Hackdustry menu → Music tab → "Show Folder"
   - Path: `<MindustryFolder>/mods/hackdustry-music/`

2. Add your music files:
   - Supported formats: `.ogg`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.flac`
   - Any song you like!

3. Rescan and play:
   - Click "Rescan" in music tab
   - Select your track and enjoy!

### 🎮 Usage

1. **Open Menu**: Tap the green **"H"** button
2. **Move Button**: Long-press and drag to reposition
3. **Select Tab**: Choose a category (Resources, Units, etc.)
4. **Use Features**: Tap any button to activate cheats
5. **Search**: Use the search bar to find specific functions
